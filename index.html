Inquiry questions and objectives撰寫探究問題與目標

A good driving question is clear, answerable with evidence, and not only yes/no.

良好的問題應清楚、可用證據作答，而不只是「是／否」。

In Hong Kong, why does ______ affect ______? What can we do / prevent?在香港，______ 為什麼會影響 ______？我們可以怎樣做／怎樣預防？

Weak vs better較弱 vs 較好

Weaker: Is internet addiction good or bad?較弱：網絡成癮是好是壞？

Better: In Hong Kong, why can heavy screen use affect Form 2 sleep and study? What can schools and families do?

較佳：在香港，過長上網為什麼會影響中二學生的睡眠與學習？學校與家庭可以怎樣預防？

Phase 2 · Cycles 7–12 (Late Term 1)階段 2 · 第 7 至 12 教節（上學期後期）

Data collection and field research收集資料與實地研究

Following teacher guidelines · Phase 2依照老師指引 Phase 2

Goal: primary and secondary research. Submit a mid-project progress log and data drafts.

目標：做初級同次級研究。要交：中期進度紀錄＋資料草稿。

Why it matters為何重要？

Your report needs reliable evidence: news/statistics/government publications, plus survey, interview, or site work.

報告要有可靠證據：新聞／統計／政府刊物，加問卷、訪問或實地。

Write the source name and date. Use your own words; do not copy whole paragraphs.

記下出處名稱與日期。用自己的句子書寫，不要整段抄錄。

Ask consent before interviewing; no classmates’ full names; no faces in field photos.

訪問前須先徵求同意；勿公開同學全名；實地考察勿拍攝人面。

Phase 3 · Cycles 13–18 (Early Term 2)階段 3 · 第 13 至 18 教節（下學期初）

Data analysis and report writing分析資料與撰寫報告

Following teacher guidelines · Phase 3依照老師指引 Phase 3

Goal: synthesise findings, evaluate social impacts, propose constructive solutions. Submit the final written report.

目標：綜合發現、評估社會影響、提出有建設性的建議。要交：最終書面報告。

Report structure: Introduction, Findings, Analysis, Recommendations, References.

報告結構：導言、發現、分析、建議、參考資料。

Submit an oral presentation (PPT and poster) plus an individual reflection log. About 8 minutes, plus Q&A and peer evaluation.

不要只寫「大家提高意識」。須說明誰（WHO）做甚麼（WHAT）。

Avoid only “everyone should be more aware”. Say WHO does WHAT.
